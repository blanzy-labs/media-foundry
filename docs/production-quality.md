# Media Foundry Production Quality v1

## Scrappy, Not Garbage

Scrappy is an intentional handmade identity: rough panels, physical motion, odd humor, and visible texture. Unreadable text, accidental silence, mismatched voice, clipped audio, weak branding, dead pacing, and abrupt edits are defects—not style.

## Audio

- Voice is the primary element. It must be intelligible, semantically aligned, intentionally cast, and classified for release eligibility.
- Music provides continuity and movement without competing with speech. Fade-in and fade-out are applied and measured on the music stem itself.
- Ducking follows narration timing with short smooth ramps. It should generally be felt rather than noticed.
- SFX must justify their presence: hook, physical motion, punchline, or reveal. Capability alone is not a reason to add a cue.
- Media Foundry v1 targets -16 LUFS integrated and no more than -1.0 dBTP for general short-form output. Current production normalization targets -1.5 dBTP for margin.
- Silence may be deliberate when declared by pacing data and supported by the surrounding mix. Accidental dead air and truncated tails fail review.

## Visuals

- Prefer constructed visuals: Godot-native scenes and project runtime assets come before Blender assets, authentic source media, and external static media. Static evidence remains appropriate when authenticity is the point.
- Persistent worlds may carry multiple semantic beats through camera, object, lighting, environmental, and physical-sign changes instead of replacing the composition.
- Text is not the video. A middle sequence should remain visually interesting and communicate action when its typography is mentally removed.
- Text remains readable inside responsive safe areas on a phone-sized display.
- Motion must have a purpose: entrance, reveal, story action, emphasis, reaction, or settle. Constant movement is not a substitute for direction.
- Authentic media takes priority over decorative framing. `contain` is preferred when cropping would remove branding, HUD, or evidence.
- Layout, crop, and motion remain deterministic and independently validated.

## Editorial

- The first two seconds establish curiosity and forward motion.
- Every beat has an objective; duration, transition, narration, media, and SFX support that objective.
- Required messages are explicitly owned by beats. Structural checks prove coverage without claiming semantic taste.
- Punchline pauses and brand reveals are deliberate timeline data.
- The product—not the studio card—is the memorable final idea. Outros remain concise and readable.

## Three Gates

1. **Technical:** deterministic production and machine validation pass.
2. **Editorial:** a human confirms communication, pacing, voice fit, music, SFX, humor, and ending quality.
3. **Release:** a human explicitly accepts the selected artifact as a public representation of the project.

Technical PASS never implies Editorial PASS. Editorial PASS never implies Release PASS. A test-only or otherwise non-release-eligible asset blocks the Release gate even when the artifact is technically valid.

## Golden Production Baseline

Only a human-approved candidate may become Media Foundry Golden Production Baseline v1. Record its creative contract, fixture, voice and asset classifications, beat timeline, music/SFX configuration, final measurements, and artifact hash. Future work must meet its intentional-quality floor without mechanically copying its settings.
