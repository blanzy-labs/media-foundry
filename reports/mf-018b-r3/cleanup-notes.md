# MF-018B-R3 Cleanup Notes

## Control-panel outline

R3 adds one continuous, closed teal perimeter following the full sloped console face. Its lower edge is routed below the indicator row, leaving all four dot housings pixel-identical to R2. The lower rectangular inner border removed in R2 was not restored.

The gauges, rotary dials, red-knob startup lever, and indicator row remain in their approved positions and retain their existing behavior.

## L-shaped artifact

The heavy L-shaped pipe protruding from the reactor's upper-left support and its highlight path were removed. No replacement object or filler geometry was added. The vertical reactor support and local background remain intact.

## Preserved scope

The R1 promo driver is reused byte-for-byte. Startup, gauges, blue/green/yellow indicator progression, linked-ring activation, reactor motion, camera motion, and audio timing are unchanged. Deterministic matched-frame comparison found no changes outside the display, console-outline, and artifact-removal regions.
