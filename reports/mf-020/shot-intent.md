# MF-020 Shot Intent

## Purpose

Create one Blender-native cinematic hero shot—not a Godot comparison and not a gameplay scene. A low perspective camera approaches and subtly orbits a central experimental reactor while the machine wakes through physical, causally linked events.

## Beginning, escalation, end

The room begins dormant with the reactor readable in silhouette and the analog console secondary in the left foreground. A physical lever engages, gauge needles rise, practical lamps transition from amber to red, pressure vents, energy forms inside the glass chamber, and the containment collar responds. Reactor light and atmospheric depth intensify until full power, followed by a 1.7-second threatening hold.

## Composition

- Hero: central glass containment reactor.
- Secondary: left-foreground console, readable but not dominant.
- Depth: foreground console, mid-ground reactor, background supports and pipe runs.
- Camera: one restrained 46 mm push with a subtle rightward orbit; no cuts.
- Text: the scene renders cleanly. Media Foundry introduces only the approved title during the final hold.

## Production stages

`CONCEPT → BLOCKOUT → DETAIL → LIGHTING → ANIMATION_FX → FINAL_RENDER → FINALIZATION`

The orchestrator must pass the deterministic blockout composition gate before it may render the detail, lighting, FX, or final stages.
