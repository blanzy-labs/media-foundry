The independent heuristic recommends `HYBRID`. Surface and illustration richness are central to matching the pulp cover, while the reactor, gauges, lamps, local lighting, steam, and print behavior still need meaningful animation.

`PLATE` would underserve the motion requirement. `PROCEDURAL` remains suitable for the reactor and instrumentation but not for inventing the entire illustrated environment. `AUTHENTIC_MEDIA` is inappropriate because the cover is art direction rather than footage or a literal scene source.

Configured and resolved strategy are both `HYBRID`; no fallback is allowed.
