# Procedural versus hybrid proof

Both proofs use the MF-016-approved hierarchy and identical four-second Godot overlay behavior: animated energy, irregular incandescent lamps, local yellow light response, sparse particles, and one steam source.

| Dimension | Procedural | Hybrid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Environmental source | MF-016 geometric composition | Generated raster development plate |
| Reactor owner | Godot | Godot |
| Local effects owner | Godot/compositor | Godot/compositor |
| Surface model | Lines, fills, deterministic grain | Painterly wear, grime, pipes, layered illustration |
| Hero/support luma ratio | 1.958 | 1.138 |
| Mean edge-gradient | 7.540 | 9.880 |
| High-frequency detail p90 | 46.715 | 69.565 |
| Quantized color count | 1,061 | 2,776 |

The procedural proof remains the approved, fair baseline rather than being intentionally degraded. The hybrid candidate visibly improves material richness, period detail, and spatial depth. Its reactor remains dominant, though less aggressively isolated than the simplified procedural graphic; human review should judge whether that balance is preferable.
