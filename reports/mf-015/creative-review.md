# MF-015 creative review

## Intended experience

The candidate uses hard editorial cuts between distressed cards and a constructed atomic-era machine room. Indicators activate one by one, analog needles rise, the reactor filaments expand, the silhouette leans back, and a short exposure overload cuts to the final printed title.

## Human review checklist

1. Does this feel like a lost 1950s science-fiction trailer?
2. Does it remain serious rather than becoming parody?
3. Does its palette, machinery, typography, and print texture belong to the cover's world?
4. Does the opening warning card establish enough mystery?
5. Does the machine visibly wake up?
6. Is the reactor escalation strong and readable?
7. Does the simple silhouette add sufficient scale and tension?
8. Do `UNKNOWN`, `UNCONTROLLED`, and `UNDISCOVERED` land effectively?
9. Does the final `UNKNOWN PROCESS — BOOK ONE` card have enough impact?
10. Is the final CTA readable without feeling like a modern promotional overlay?
11. Does the temporary score support the concept, or should it be replaced before refinement?

## Recommendation

Refine this pulp-trailer format before testing another pulp concept, provided human review agrees with the core tone. The technical proof already demonstrates reusable cards, machine activation, escalation, character scale, reactor peak, and deterministic analog treatment. The highest-value next pass would improve illustration richness and secure an approved permanent score rather than branching immediately into a second visual grammar.

If the human review rejects the seriousness, machinery style, or title-card pacing, preserve MF-015 as a capability test and test the next concept instead of polishing the wrong direction.
