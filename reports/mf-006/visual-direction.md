# MF-006 Visual Direction

**Strategy:** Godot-generated low-resolution science-fiction chamber. The approved Dark Signal cover was inspected only for dark blue/cyan/orange atmosphere and title hierarchy; no cover pixels, texture, screenshot, or external artwork are loaded by the fixture or renderer.

The 540×960 scene contains battered panels, hanging cables, crude LEDs, moving circuit paths, an electronic platform, burst particles, a generated cover/spine/page block, three independent page states, camera reactions, data fragments, and an in-world CTA. It is upscaled to 1080×1920 with nearest-neighbor sampling.
