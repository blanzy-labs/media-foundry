# MF-018A Comparison Notes

## MF-017 hybrid versus MF-018A native

The comparison is deliberately not scored as a blanket win for either source strategy.

MF-017 retains the stronger painterly advantages:

- substantially richer industrial detail and surface materiality;
- deeper environmental density;
- closer visual fidelity to the pulp-cover art direction.

MF-018A demonstrates native-scene advantages:

- energy is clipped to, and visibly grows inside, the physical chamber;
- warning lamps occupy drawn housings on the reactor ring and console;
- gauge needles rotate around stable native pivots;
- reactor, console, background, atmosphere, and camera push share one transform graph;
- there is no plate/overlay registration boundary;
- machine motion is deterministic and easier to parameterize.

The native candidate is deliberately simpler. Human review must decide whether its improved coherence and repeatability compensate for reduced painterly richness.
