# MF-015R1 comparison to MF-015

## Preserved

- Complete 30-second editorial structure and hard-cut timing.
- Opening warning and `UNKNOWN`, `UNCONTROLLED`, `UNDISCOVERED` order.
- Machine wake-up, charge, escalation, overload, and final-title logic.
- Exact palette, typography, card wording, author, and CTA.
- Seed and source-cover reference.
- Temporary score, processing, and decoded PCM content.

## Character improvement

MF-015 used a circular head, single angular coat mass, and one heavy arm. MF-015R1 adds a profile with brow/nose/chin, neck, lapels, coat folds, separated legs and feet, rear arm, bent reactor-side arm, elbow joint, palm, and fingers.

| Region | MF-015 midtone ratio | MF-015R1 midtone ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Head | 0.229 | 0.819 |
| Torso | 0.076 | 0.830 |
| Hand region | 0.367 | 0.846 |
| Legs | 0.595 | 0.661 |

Character reaction changed-pixel coverage rises from `0.14572` to `0.30103`, while remaining bounded below the validator's over-animation threshold.

## Material and environment improvement

| Region | MF-015 texture gradient | MF-015R1 | Increase |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Background | 3.642 | 4.109 | 12.8% |
| Machine room | 3.748 | 4.259 | 13.6% |

R1 adds paper fibers, ink dropout, grime marks, scratches, deeper industrial silhouettes, nested pipes, cross-bracing, cables, and selective registration separation.

## Reactor improvement

Seven irregular plasma bodies, asymmetric orange filaments, broader light spill, and additional containment structures make the reactor less sterile. Charge-state asymmetry rises from `23.341` to `29.174`; refined luminance progresses monotonically from `71.723` to `155.115` at peak.

## Readability

All measured card contrasts remain high. The largest reduction is the intentionally more distressed `UNCONTROLLED` card, which remains at `75.964` contrast and `208` glyph p90.
