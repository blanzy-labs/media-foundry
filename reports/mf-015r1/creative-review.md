1. Does the figure now read immediately as a person?
2. Are the head profile, coat, limbs, hand, and reaction clear without becoming over-detailed?
3. Does the restrained recoil add tension rather than distract from the reactor?
4. Does the room feel more like a battered pulp illustration?
5. Do fibers, grime, scratches, and registration drift feel authentic rather than noisy?
6. Does the added machinery layering create convincing depth without clutter?
7. Is the asymmetric plasma stranger and more atmospheric?
8. Does the unchanged trailer structure remain punchy?
9. Is MF-015R1 clearly preferable to MF-015?
10. Is the visual family ready to support another pulp-video concept?

## Recommendation

If human review prefers R1 in the A/B comparison, freeze this as the refined visual grammar and move to the next pulp concept. The primary weaknesses identified in MF-015 now have measurable and visible improvements; another immediate polish pass risks diminishing returns.

Treat permanent music approval as a separate production prerequisite. If the character still feels too diagrammatic or the added depth feels cluttered, perform one narrowly scoped character/foreground refinement before expanding the family.
